Stock Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Stock Tracker is a web app that field technicians use to log the equipment they receive, deploy, transfer, send on RMA and recycle. This page explains what information it handles and where that information goes.

Information you enter

You enter your name, your dealership, equipment serial numbers, customer account numbers, RMA numbers, transfer details and notes. When you allow it, the app also records the GPS location of the phone at the time of each entry.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored on your own phone, in your browser's storage for this website. Stock Tracker has no server and no database. The developer of Stock Tracker does not receive, collect or see any of your information.

If you choose to save an Excel backup, the file is created on your phone and saved wherever you choose, such as your OneDrive.

Google Sheets (optional)

If you connect Google Sheets, the app signs in to your own Google account and keeps a copy of your entries in a spreadsheet in your Google Drive. It requests only two permissions:

  • drive.file lets the app create and update the one spreadsheet it makes for you. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.
  • userinfo.email lets the app show which Google account is connected.

Your information goes directly from your phone to Google. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. Stock Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time from the Backup tab in the app, and remove the app's access in your Google Account under Security, then Third-party connections. Your spreadsheet stays in your Google Drive until you delete it.

Sharing

Stock Tracker does not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual entries in the app. To remove everything from your phone, clear this website's data in your browser settings or remove the app from your home screen. Delete your spreadsheet or backup files in Google Drive or OneDrive to remove those copies.
